ℹ️ Individual Player Tracker
This software is designed to track and gamify the season for a single player. It is not built to track rosters of multiple players. Team stats entered are solely used to calculate your personal advanced metrics (like Usage %).

App Documentation

Reference guide for metrics and app workflow.

📱 App Workflow
💡 PRO-TIP: The Tracker tab is fully optimized for quick logging on your mobile phone during live games. However, because of the massive amount of column data, the History tab is best viewed on a tablet or desktop PC for deep stat analysis.
  • 1. Setup: Go to the Settings tab to hide/show stats, reorder the Tracker layout, or set Default Team Minutes.
  • 2. Entry: Log game details and use the +/- buttons to track stats. Made shots automatically add an attempt.
  • 3. Save: Hit Save to push the game to your History log.
  • 4. Review & Trash: Go to History to view averages, edit past games (✏️), or mark blowout games as Trash to exclude them from averages.
📈 Advanced Metrics
2PT% / 3PT% / FT%: Tracks situational shooting percentages grouped tightly with raw conversion counters.

eFG% (Effective Field Goal %): Adjusts standard FG% to account for the fact that 3-point shots are worth 50% more than 2-point shots.

TS% (True Shooting %): A measure of shooting efficiency that takes into account field goals, 3-point field goals, and free throws.

USG% (Usage Percentage): An estimate of the percentage of team plays used by a player while they were on the floor.

A/TO: Assist to Turnover Ratio.
