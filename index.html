ℹ️ Individual Player Tracker
This software is designed to track and gamify the season for a single player[cite: 4]. It is not built to track rosters of multiple players[cite: 4]. Team stats entered are solely used to calculate your personal advanced metrics (like Usage %)[cite: 4].
📱 App Workflow
💡 PRO-TIP: The Tracker tab is fully optimized for quick logging on your mobile phone during live games[cite: 4]. However, because of the massive amount of column data, the History tab is best viewed on a tablet or desktop PC for deep stat analysis[cite: 4].
  • 1. Setup: Go to the Settings tab to hide/show stats, reorder the Tracker layout, or set Default Team Minutes[cite: 4]. Tap any price in the Tracker to edit its value[cite: 4].
  • 2. Entry: Log game details and use the +/- buttons to track stats[cite: 4]. Made shots automatically add an attempt[cite: 4].
  • 3. Save: Hit Save to push the game to your History log[cite: 4].
  • 4. Review & Trash: Go to History to view averages, edit past games (✏️), or mark blowout games as Trash (🗑️) to exclude them from averages[cite: 4]. To restore a trashed game, check the "Trash" box at the top of the History tab to reveal hidden games, then click the Restore icon (♻️)[cite: 4].
  • 5. Export PDF: Tap the ✏️ icon on any game in the History tab and click Export PDF to download a printable box score[cite: 4].
📋 Game Details (Header)
  • Season: Type a new season or pick from the dropdown[cite: 4]. The app automatically defaults to your most recently created season[cite: 4].
  • Scores & Result: Enter "Our" score and "Opp" (Opponent) score[cite: 4]. The Win/Tie/Loss toggle will automatically update based on the scores you enter[cite: 4].
  • Minutes (MIN): Enter Player MIN (your playing time) and Team MIN (total game length, e.g., 32, 40, or 48)[cite: 4]. Team MIN is required to accurately calculate Usage Percentage (USG%)[cite: 4]. You can set your default Team MIN in the Settings tab so you don't have to enter it every time[cite: 4].
  • Location: Toggle whether the game was Home or Away[cite: 4].
💸 Custom Metrics
Total $: An incentive option for players to earn money by accumulating stats[cite: 4]. Prices are editable by tapping the blue price text in the Tracker[cite: 4].

Trash Flag: Indicates if the game was a blowout or garbage time[cite: 4]. Toggling this in History removes the game from your season averages[cite: 4].
📈 Advanced Metrics
eFG% (Effective Field Goal %): Adjusts standard FG% to account for the fact that 3-point shots are worth 50% more than 2-point shots[cite: 4].

TS% (True Shooting %): A measure of shooting efficiency that takes into account field goals, 3-point field goals, and free throws[cite: 4].

USG% (Usage Percentage): An estimate of the percentage of team plays used by a player while they were on the floor[cite: 4]. Requires Team MIN, Team FGA, Team FTA, and Team TO to calculate[cite: 4].

A/TO: Assist to Turnover Ratio[cite: 4].
