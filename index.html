ℹ️ Individual Player Tracker
This software is designed to track and gamify the season for a single player. It is not built to track rosters of multiple players. Team stats entered are solely used to calculate your personal advanced metrics (like Usage %).
📱 App Workflow
💡 PRO-TIP: The Tracker tab is fully optimized for quick logging on your mobile phone during live games. However, because of the massive amount of column data, the History tab is best viewed on a tablet or desktop PC for deep stat analysis.
  • 1. Setup: Go to the Settings tab to hide/show stats, reorder the Tracker layout, or set Default Team Minutes. Tap any price in the Tracker to edit its value.
  • 2. Entry: Log game details and use the +/- buttons to track stats. Made shots automatically add an attempt.
  • 3. Save: Hit Save to push the game to your History log.
  • 4. Review & Trash: Go to History to view averages, edit past games (✏️), or mark blowout games as Trash (🗑️) to exclude them from averages. To restore a trashed game, check the "Trash" box at the top of the History tab to reveal hidden games, then click the Restore icon (♻️).
  • 5. Export PDF: Tap the ✏️ icon on any game in the History tab and click Export PDF to download a printable box score.
📋 Game Details (Header)
  • Season: Type a new season or pick from the dropdown. The app automatically defaults to your most recently created season.
  • Scores & Result: Enter "Our" score and "Opp" (Opponent) score. The Win/Tie/Loss toggle will automatically update based on the scores you enter.
  • Minutes (MIN): Enter Player MIN (your playing time) and Team MIN (total game length, e.g., 32, 40, or 48). Team MIN is required to accurately calculate Usage Percentage (USG%). You can set your default Team MIN in the Settings tab so you don't have to enter it every time.
  • Location: Toggle whether the game was Home or Away.
💸 Custom Metrics
Total $: An incentive option for players to earn money by accumulating stats. Prices are editable by tapping the blue price text in the Tracker.

Advanced Calculations Reference:
  • Usage Rate (USG%): Measures the percentage of team plays concluded by the player while on the court. Formulated using field goal attempts, free throw attempts, and turnovers against total team counts.
  • Effective Field Goal Percentage (eFG%): Adjusts normal field goal percentages to account for the fact that 3-point field goals are worth more than 2-point field goals. Built cleanly as: (2PT Made + 1.5 * 3PT Made) / (Total Field Goal Attempts).
